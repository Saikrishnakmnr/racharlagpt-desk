BUILT FOR REAL USE

Simple rules, clear expectations.

Do I need an account?

No. The first version is designed without login, registration or a user database.

Are my uploaded files stored?

The Desk is designed around browser-first processing for supported operations. It does not create a user history database. Sensitive workflows should still tell users exactly when a network service is required.

Does every feature require AI?

No. Cleaning, formatting, extraction checks and presentation structure can be deterministic. Optional AI can improve language-heavy tasks without becoming the single point of failure.

Is the hospital-bill result official?

No. It is an AI-assisted/document-processing aid for understanding and verification. Users should compare important information with the original document and seek professional advice when appropriate.

PRIVACY

Privacy by design.

RacharlaGPT Desk is designed without user accounts, profiles or a database in this first version. Supported browser-first tools process files locally in the browser. If a future feature needs a network service, the interface should clearly tell the user before sending data.

Do not upload sensitive documents to any online service unless you understand where the data goes and why it is needed.

TERMS

Use the Desk responsibly.

The Desk provides utilities and generated drafts for convenience. You are responsible for reviewing outputs, checking important facts, respecting copyrights and using generated content appropriately.

Features, limits and external services may change as the product evolves.

DISCLAIMER

Important information.

AI-generated or automated results may contain mistakes. They are provided for general understanding and awareness and are not medical, legal, financial or professional advice. They are not official documents or proof of a claim or dispute. Verify important information against the original source and consult a qualified professional when appropriate.
